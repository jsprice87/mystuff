Uniqueness Rules & Assumptions

  • Board: 10 wide × 15 tall = 150 cells
  • Pieces: 8 types × 5 copies = 40 pieces total
  • Mirror images: Horizontal and vertical reflections of a solution are NOT counted as unique
  • Identical pieces: Swapping two pieces of the same type (e.g., two green squares) does NOT create a new unique solution
  • Rotations: Since the board is not square (10×15), 90° rotations produce a different board shape and are not applicable
  • Piece flipping: Physical pieces can be flipped over, so both sides of each piece are usable (all orientations considered)
